Questions

Common questions, honest answers

How does the free trial work?↓

Sign in with Google and start your 3-month free trial — everything is unlocked, with no card or UPI needed, and you are never charged automatically. After the trial you can continue with Premium (₹39/month or ₹468/year) or stay on the free plan.

How long does each level take?↓

Each level covers one academic year with weekly 20–30 minute sessions. One new module unlocks each month. Students who finish early get excellence challenges.

What are WealthPoints and XP?↓

WealthPoints are earned for attending classes and completing activities. XP (Experience Points) measure your overall progress. Both contribute to your city leaderboard rank.

Is there a scholarship available?↓

Yes. Any student can apply with a 100-word story about why they want to learn about money, or submit income proof. We review within 3 days and grant full free access to all 8 levels.

What devices does it work on?↓

Any device with a browser — phone, tablet, or computer. No app download required. The campus works on all modern browsers.
